1. What HabitForge is

HabitForge turns real-life tasks into an RPG-style growth loop: you complete quests (habits, dailies and to-dos), earn EXP and gold, level up a character, unlock achievements and spend rewards in the Forge shop. The app is local-first: by default all game data is stored on your device and no account is required.

HabitForge does not provide medical, psychological, fitness, or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for professional care. Reminders and streaks are motivational tools only.

3. Accounts and data modes

HabitForge supports three data modes, selected at runtime through the app's configuration:

ModeWhat it means for you
Local (default) No account. Game data lives in local on-device storage. Sign-in is skipped (guest mode).
Cloud (Firebase) Sign in with Google (and, where enabled, Apple). Authentication and cloud storage are provided by Firebase/Google services under their own terms.
Self-hosted server Sign in with email and password against a backend operated by you or your provider. Game data is exchanged with that backend.

You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and for the security of any backend you operate yourself. We may suspend access to cloud or server features if we reasonably believe they are being abused.
