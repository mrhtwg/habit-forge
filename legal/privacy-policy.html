Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026  ·  Play without an account, or sign in to sync — you choose

Short version. HabitForge is built to work without an account: if you don't sign in, your quests and game progress stay on your device. The current build contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not sell personal data. You can delete your data at any time from Settings.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for HabitForge is HabitForge Studio, located in the People's Republic of China. You can reach us at habitforge.official@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

2. Data the app processes

CategoryExamples in HabitForge
Task content Quest titles, task type (habit / daily / to-do), difficulty, tags, due dates, repeat weekdays, streaks, completion and skip history, HP penalties.
Game profile Character class, level, EXP, HP, allocated attribute points, equipped items, owned items, unlocked achievements, gold and gem balances.
Preferences Language/locale, sound and haptic toggles, onboarding class choice, session state.
Identifiers A randomly generated character identifier, and — if you sign in — an account identifier and a session token issued by the sign-in service.
Account details If you sign in with an email address, that email address. Passwords are never stored by the app; only the resulting session token is kept on your device so you stay signed in.

HabitForge does not ask for your real name, phone number, postal address, or payment details, and the app has no social or sharing features.

3. Where your data is stored

It depends on one simple choice you make in the app — whether you sign in:

If you don't sign in

  • All task, character, preference and wallet data is stored only on your device.
  • No account is created and nothing is transmitted to us. The app works fully offline.
  • Data is removed when you reset it in Settings or uninstall the app.

If you sign in

  • You can sign in with a third-party account (Google or Apple, where available) or with an email address and password.
  • Your game data is then associated with that account and kept on the sign-in provider's or our backend's servers so it can be restored and synced across your devices.
  • The app stores the session token in its private app storage on your device so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

4. What HabitForge does not collect

  • No contacts, calendar, SMS or call data.
  • No photos, videos, or files from your gallery.
  • No microphone or camera access.
  • No precise or background location.
  • No health, fitness or medical records.
  • No advertising identifier, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current build.
  • No payment card or billing information (the app has no real-money purchases).

5. Why we process data

  • To run the game loop you asked for — storing quests, computing rewards, tracking streaks, penalties, levels and shop purchases (performance of a contract).
  • To authenticate you and sync your data when you sign in (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the app and its services secure and to prevent abuse or tampering (legitimate interests).
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use your task content for advertising or sell it to anyone.

6. Device permissions

  • Network access — used only when you sign in or sync. Without an account the app needs no network connection at all.
  • Audio playback — the app plays short sound effects for rewards and level-ups; it never records audio.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification permissions in the current build.

7. Sharing and disclosures

HabitForge shares data only in these situations:

  • With the sign-in provider you choose — Google or Apple — and with our backend service that stores your synced game data when you have an account.
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the public.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Retention

  • Without an account: your data is kept on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app.
  • With an account: your synced data is kept while your account is active; you can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.
  • Session token: deleted from the device when you sign out.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, including transport encryption for data sent to our services. Please note the honest limits of a mobile app: locally stored game data and the session token are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which is not additionally encrypted by HabitForge, so device-level security (screen lock, OS encryption) matters. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. In practice, inside the app you can:

  • Access and edit your tasks, character, preferences and equipment at any time;
  • Delete everything with Settings → Reset all data (this permanently clears quests, character, items, achievements and virtual balances);
  • Sign out to remove the local session token;
  • Uninstall the app, which removes locally stored data from the device.

If you have an account, contact habitforge.official@gmail.com to request account deletion or a copy of your data. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

HabitForge is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

If you use an account, your synced data may be processed on servers located outside your country, under the safeguards our service providers offer. Without an account, nothing leaves your device.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app gains features (for example, new sign-in options or new platform services). Material changes will be published here with a new effective date.
