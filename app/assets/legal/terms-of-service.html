1. What HabitForge is

HabitForge turns real-life tasks into an RPG-style growth loop: you complete quests (habits, dailies and to-dos), earn EXP and gold, level up a character, unlock achievements and spend rewards in the Forge shop. The app is local-first: by default all game data is stored on your device and no account is required.

HabitForge does not provide medical, psychological, fitness, or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for professional care. Reminders and streaks are motivational tools only.
